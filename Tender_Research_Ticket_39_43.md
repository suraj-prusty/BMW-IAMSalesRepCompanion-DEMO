Based on the BMW IAM Tender documentation and supporting project materials, the strongest external benchmarks for Work Packages / Tickets 39-43 are:

# Final Recommendation

For BMW IAM Tickets 39-43, the strongest strategic benchmark is not a single vendor.

The ideal future-state benchmark is:

PartsTrader
+
Solera
+
Tekion

Where:

- Solera identifies vehicle repair needs.
- PartsTrader powers repair-to-parts procurement.
- Tekion provides the unified AI-native operating platform.
- Cox Automotive provides market intelligence.
- CDK provides governance and dealer KPI frameworks.

This combination most closely aligns with BMW IAM objectives around collision growth, dealer enablement, Independent Repairer engagement, PL24 adoption, analytics-driven decision making, and future Agentic AI opportunities.
